Submission Guidelines

Share your predicted queries and database execution results.

How to submit

To submit to the TQTS-Bench leaderboard, send your files as a compressed archive or a downloadable link to lex2m673@gmail.com.

Use the email subject [TQTS-Bench Submission] <method name>. Results will be reviewed before being added to the leaderboard.

What to include

  • Predicted queries: one final query for each question, clearly identified by its benchmark package and question ID.
  • Execution results: the complete outputs obtained by running those queries on the corresponding benchmark databases. Match each output to its query and identify failures or skipped questions.
  • A short method description: your preferred leaderboard display name, model or method used, benchmark version, evaluated scope, and any departures from the standard evaluation setting. Self-evaluated scores are optional.

No database dumps, source datasets, or reference answers are required.

Submission notes

  • Preserve the execution outputs and use the benchmark's database contents and reference time settings.
  • Submit one final result per question. Disclose manual intervention or use of reference answers; do not manually select predictions based on their correctness.
  • Clearly label partial evaluations. Do not omit failed questions from the declared evaluation scope.
  • You may use an anonymous method name. Do not include credentials, private connection details, or identifying metadata.
